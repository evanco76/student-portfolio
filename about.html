About me

A compassionate doctor who is learning to protect patients beyond the ward: in the data that describes them.

Who I am

I am a visionary and compassionate doctor. I am unassuming, dedicated and hardworking, and I let my work speak for me.

How I found cybersecurity

My interest began with my own experience of data breaches while using the internet. It motivated me to explore cybersecurity properly and to enrol in the programme.

What sets me apart

As a medical doctor, I see how sensitive patient information is. The world is moving towards electronic medical records (EMR), so protecting patients’ healthcare data must be a priority. I bring a clinical view and a security view together, so that patient information is protected from vulnerability.
